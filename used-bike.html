COMPARE THE RIGHT THINGS

Price Is Only One Part of Value

A cheaper bike can cost more later if tyres, brakes, insurance or service are due soon. Compare readiness, not only price.

Upcoming maintenance expense Insurance validity Tyre and brake life Service record quality
SHORTLIST SMARTLY

Questions to Ask Every Seller

Why are you selling?

The answer can provide useful context about usage and urgency.

What was recently serviced?

Know whether major consumables or scheduled services are already handled.

Any accident or repair history?

Ask specifically about frame, fork, wheel and major body repairs.

Are all documents current?

Confirm RC, insurance, loan closure and ownership details before payment.

PRICE UNDERSTANDING

What Changes a Used Bike's Value?

Model Year

Newer model years usually retain more value when condition is comparable.

Kilometres

Usage matters, but maintenance quality can matter just as much.

Owner Count

Fewer owners can improve buyer confidence when documents and history are clear.

Local Demand

Popular models can command different resale values across cities and regions.

TEST RIDE ESSENTIALS

Use the Ride to Confirm the Listing

Listen to the cold start, feel the clutch, test brakes and check stability. A short ride can reveal what photos cannot.
