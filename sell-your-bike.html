SELLING PROCESS

Four Steps From Bike to Buyer

1. Add Details

Enter model, year, kilometres, ownership and location accurately.

2. Present the Bike

Upload clear photos and explain condition, service history and upgrades.

3. Handle Enquiries

Speak with interested buyers and discuss price with the facts already visible.

4. Complete Transfer

Finish payment, handover and ownership transfer using proper documentation.

BETTER LISTING CONTENT

What Serious Buyers Want to Know

Actual Usage

Share honest kilometres and the kind of riding the motorcycle was used for.

Maintenance

Mention recent services, tyre changes, battery replacement and major consumables.

Insurance & RC

Explain document validity and whether the motorcycle has active finance.

Upgrades & Modifications

List meaningful accessories or modifications clearly instead of hiding them.

PHOTO GUIDE

Good Photos Build Faster Trust

Use daylight, a clean background and clear angles. Show both sides, tyres, cluster, engine area and visible damage.

VALUATION FACTORS

What Affects Your Resale Price?

Age

Model year and registration age influence depreciation.

Kilometres

Lower kilometres can support value when the bike is well maintained.

Condition

Mechanical health, tyres, paint, plastics and crash history all matter.

Market Demand

Popular models, variants and colours may sell more easily in some cities.

PREPARE BEFORE LISTING

Small Improvements Can Make a Big Difference

Clean Properly

A clean motorcycle is easier to inspect and presents better in photos.

Fix Basic Maintenance

Address obvious issues such as low fluids, loose chain or weak battery.

Collect Records

Keep RC, insurance, service invoices and loan-closure paperwork ready.

Set a Realistic Ask

Price with room for negotiation, but stay aligned with age and condition.

SAFE SELLING

Protect Yourself During the Deal

Meet safely, verify the buyer, protect sensitive documents, confirm payment and complete ownership transfer promptly.

SELLER QUESTIONS

Common Questions Before You List

Should I service first?

Fix obvious maintenance items if they materially affect ride quality or buyer confidence.

Can I sell with finance?

Outstanding finance generally needs proper closure before clean ownership transfer.

How much should I negotiate?

Use condition and comparable listings to decide a realistic minimum selling price.
